Basic Strategy Reference

Every seat plays this exact strategy, including the tracked player — no human-controlled play decisions anywhere in the app. Transcribed by hand from BlackjackStreakSimulator.Engine/BasicStrategy.cs — see BASIC_STRATEGY.md for the plain-text version (the one that renders natively when browsing the repo on GitHub).

House rules: dealer hits soft 17 · double down on any two cards · double after split (DAS) allowed · resplit up to 4 hands total, except Aces (never resplit, one card each, no further hitting).
Legend: H = Hit · S = Stand · D = Double (see note) · Sp = Split · columns are the dealer's up card.

  • 10-value pairs never split, regardless of rank — 20 is already a stand.
  • 5,5 never splits either — it's played as a hard 10 instead.
  • 6,6 splits only against 2–6 — a stricter cutoff than 2,2 / 3,3 / 7,7, which split against 2–7. Easy to conflate since they look like the same shape of rule.
  • A pair otherwise eligible to split won't be offered Split once a seat already has 4 hands for the round, or if this hand is itself a split Ace (which never resplits) — it falls through to the hard/soft table for its total instead.
